The Soundtrack of Your Life

Music preferences · Present simple · Listening, reading, writing and speaking · A1–A2

Welcome!

By the end of this workshop, students will be able to express music preferences using at least 10 music-related words, form simple present-tense sentences, understand a short text, and take part in a brief dialogue about a favorite song or singer.

Instructions
  1. Open each activity in the sequence below.
  2. Read the instructions and complete the task.
  3. Use feedback to review your answers.
  4. For listening, press Play or show the transcript.
  5. For speaking, recording is optional.
